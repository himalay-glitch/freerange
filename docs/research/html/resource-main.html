Amplify: An Innovation Workshop

Get the resource

Is your organization doing everything it can for you? What's missing from your approach? How can you deliver more value to your audience, your team members, and yourself—and make a change that changes everything else?

Our Amplify workshop is designed with these and other big-picture questions in mind. A powerful idea engine, this exciting, jam-packed working session is crammed with activities that will help you and your team create strategies for change in key areas of your organization.

By the end of this workshop, you and your team will have a robust, actionable plan for capturing much more value out of your organization—and sustaining an experience audiences will love.

To learn more about Amplify, please download our workshop information packet. If you have any questions or would like to sign up for a session, please email us at ideas@freerange.com. We'd love to help you see your work in a whole new light.
